💡 Quick Agronomy Tips

  • Always test soil pH before planting: optimal range for most crops is 6.0 to 7.2.
  • Split Nitrogen: Avoid dumping all nitrogen as basal; apply in 2-3 splits at active vegetative stages.
  • Basal Phosphorus: Apply 100% Phosphorus at sowing near the root zone for vigorous root architecture.
  • Water management: Drain standing water 10-15 days prior to grain harvest.
