# Complete Rice (Paddy) Cultivation Dossier

## 1. Land & Soil Suitability
- **Optimal Soil Type**: Heavy clayey loams and alluvial silty soils capable of retaining surface water.
- **Ideal pH Range**: 5.5 to 6.8 (tolerant to slightly acidic conditions).
- **Land Preparation**: Deep summer plowing followed by 2-3 passes of wet puddling to break large aggregates and form an impervious layer (plow sole) at 15-20 cm depth to minimize percolation loss.

## 2. Sowing & Nursery
- **Nursery Methods**: Wet bed, dry bed, or Modified Mat Nursery (SRI technique).
- **Seed Treatment**: Soak seeds in brine solution (10%) to remove unfilled seeds; treat with Carbendazim (2g/kg) and Azospirillum (600g/ha).
- **Transplanting**: Transplant 21-25 day old seedlings at 2-3 seedlings per hill, spaced 20cm x 15cm.

## 3. Fertilization & Nutrient Schedule
- **Standard NPK Dosage**: 120 kg N : 60 kg P2O5 : 40 kg K2O per hectare.
- **Basal Application**: 100% Phosphorus, 50% Potassium, and 25% Nitrogen applied at final puddling.
- **Top Dressing**: 50% Nitrogen at 21-25 days (active tillering); remaining 25% N and 50% K at panicle initiation (45-50 days).
- **Micronutrient**: 25 kg Zinc Sulfate (ZnSO4) basal prevents Khaira disease.

## 4. Water Management & Balancing
- Keep 2-3 cm water layer for first 10 days post transplanting.
- Increase to 5 cm during tillering to panicle emergence.
- Alternate wetting and drying (AWD) can save 25-30% irrigation water without reducing yield.
- Drain standing water 10-14 days before harvest.

## 5. Crop Balancing & Pest Control
- **Brown Plant Hopper (BPH)**: Create 'alleyways' every 2-3 meters for aeration; spray neem formulation (Azadirachtin 1500 ppm).
- **Rice Blast**: Avoid excessive nitrogenous fertilizer; spray Tricyclazole 75 WP at 0.6g/L.
- **Crop Rotation**: Rotate with Chickpea, Lentil, or Sesbania (green manure) to rebuild soil structure and nitrogen reserves.
