# Soil Balancing, pH Correction & NPK Nutrition Guide

## 1. Soil pH Diagnostics and Rebalancing
Soil pH regulates the bioavailability of essential plant macronutrients and micronutrients:

| Soil pH Range | Condition | Availability Impact | Correction Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **< 5.5** | Strongly Acidic | Aluminum/Manganese toxicity, Phosphorus lockup | Apply Agricultural Limestone (CaCO3) or Dolomite at 1.5 - 3.0 tonnes/ha |
| **6.0 - 7.2** | Ideal / Neutral | Optimal availability of N, P, K, Ca, Mg, S, Fe, Zn | Maintain with compost, bio-fertilizers, and balanced cropping |
| **> 7.8** | Alkaline / Sodic | Zinc, Iron, Manganese, Phosphorus deficiences | Apply Agricultural Gypsum (CaSO4) + Elemental Sulfur (200-400 kg/ha) + Green Manure |

## 2. NPK Nutrient Functions and Balance
- **Nitrogen (N)**: Vegetative growth, chlorophyll formation, and leafy vigor.
  - *Deficiency*: Pale yellow leaves starting from older bottom foliage.
  - *Remedy*: Urea (46% N), Ammonium Sulfate, or Vermicompost.
- **Phosphorus (P)**: Root architecture, early root development, flowering, and seed set.
  - *Deficiency*: Dark purplish-bronze discoloration on stems and undersides of leaves.
  - *Remedy*: Single Super Phosphate (SSP - 16% P2O5), DAP (18:46:0), or Rock Phosphate.
- **Potassium (K)**: Disease resistance, stomatal regulation, fruit sizing, starch synthesis, and drought tolerance.
  - *Deficiency*: Chlorosis followed by brown scorch and curling of leaf tips and margins.
  - *Remedy*: Muriate of Potash (MOP - 60% K2O) or Potassium Sulfate (SOP - 50% K2O + 17% S).

## 3. Organic Soil Restoratives
- **Farm Yard Manure (FYM)**: 10 - 15 tonnes/ha adds humic substances and improves moisture retention.
- **Vermicompost**: Rich in plant growth hormones (auxins, gibberellins) and beneficial mycorrhizae.
- **Green Manuring**: Grow Sunnhemp (*Crotalaria juncea*) or Dhaincha (*Sesbania aculeata*) for 45 days and incorporate before planting. Adds 60-80 kg organic N/ha.
