# Tomato Production & Health Balancing Masterclass

## 1. Land & Soil Suitability
- **Optimal Soil Type**: Sandy loam rich in decomposed organic matter, red loam with deep topsoil.
- **Ideal pH Range**: 6.0 to 6.8.
- **Drainage**: Excellent drainage required. Use 15-20 cm raised beds to avoid root rot.

## 2. Growth Protocol
- **Nursery**: 25-30 days on seedling plug trays with coco-peat + vermicompost.
- **Field Spacing**: 60 cm between rows, 45 cm between plants for bush types; 90cm x 60cm for staked vining varieties.
- **Staking**: Staking with bamboo stakes or trellis wire improves yield by 40% and prevents soil-borne diseases.

## 3. Nutrient Schedule & Fertigation
- **Total Requirement**: 180 N : 100 P2O5 : 150 K2O kg/ha.
- **Calcium Requirement**: Calcium Nitrate (10-15 kg/ha) is critical to prevent Blossom End Rot.
- **Boron Spray**: 1 g/L Borax at flower initiation prevents flower drop and enhances fruit set.

## 4. Pest & Disease Balancing
- **Whitefly & Leaf Curl Virus**: Yellow sticky traps (20/acre); spray systemic insecticide or neem oil at early signs.
- **Bacterial Wilt**: Solarize soil; use resistant rootstocks or biocontrol with Trichoderma viride.
- **Companion Crop**: Plant African Marigold borders to attract bollworm moths away from tomatoes and kill root nematodes.
