# Wheat Field Management & Nutrition Dossier

## 1. Land & Soil Suitability
- **Optimal Soil Type**: Well-drained fertile loam, clay loam, and alluvial soils.
- **Ideal pH Range**: 6.0 to 7.5.
- **Drainage**: Highly critical; wheat roots suffocate in standing water within 24-48 hours.

## 2. Growth Phases & Critical Stages
1. **Crown Root Initiation (CRI)**: 20-25 days after sowing - MOST CRITICAL irrigation.
2. **Tillering**: 40-45 days.
3. **Jointing**: 60-65 days.
4. **Flowering / Anthesis**: 80-85 days.
5. **Milking**: 100-105 days.
6. **Dough Stage**: 115-120 days.

## 3. Fertilizer Formulation
- **Standard Dose**: 120 N : 60 P2O5 : 40 K2O (kg/ha).
- Apply full P & K with 1/2 N at sowing using seed-cum-fertilizer drill.
- Top-dress 1/4th N at CRI with first irrigation, and 1/4th N at tillering with second irrigation.

## 4. Nutrient Balancing & Deficiencies
- **Sulfur Deficit**: Yellowing of top leaves (unlike Nitrogen which yellows lower leaves). Apply 200 kg Gypsum/ha.
- **Yellow Rust Prevention**: Resistant cultivars (HD-2967, DBW-187, PBW-550) or Propiconazole 25 EC spray.
